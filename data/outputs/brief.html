2. Recurring Operational Pollution Patterns (Historical Ground Truth)

Diurnal Morning Peak

Across Mumbai monitoring stations, PM2.5 concentrations typically peak at 08:00 with a median of 57.8 µg/m³, which is 15.1% higher than the overall median of 50.2 µg/m³.

Peak Hour: 08:00 IST | Uplift: +15.1%

Calm-Wind Stagnation

During calm-wind stagnation episodes (wind speed <= 4.8 km/h lasting at least 3 hours), average PM2.5 rises to 58.9 µg/m³ compared to 49.2 µg/m³ during ventilated periods (+19.7% difference across 3040 episode observations).

Calm Threshold: ≤4.8 km/h | Uplift: +19.7%

Persistent Worst Corridors

Over the last 91 days, the stations with the most frequent Unhealthy air (>60 µg/m³) were Kherwadi_Bandra East, Mumbai - MPCB (46.7% of 1779 valid hours), Byculla, Mumbai - BMC (43.3% of 1599 valid hours), and Bandra Kurla Complex, Mumbai - MPCB (38.8% of 1749 valid hours).

4. Governance, Provenance & Known Limitations

Governing Limitations: Next-24-hour wind speed and rainfall features use historical observations as numerical weather prediction (NWP) stand-ins. 80% prediction intervals are conformal-calibrated to achieve 80.16% empirical coverage on out-of-time tests.
